Technology, power & the public good

A people’s
tech future.

Dr Michael Kwet is a Senior Researcher at the Centre for Social Change, University of Johannesburg, and a former Visiting Fellow of the Information Society Project at Yale Law (2017-2024). His work examines digital colonialism, surveillance, and the relationship between technology and the ecological crisis.

He is the author of Digital Degrowth: Technology in the Age of Survival and editor of The Cambridge Handbook of Race and Surveillance. He received his PhD in Sociology from Rhodes University.

Dr Kwet's writing has appeared in The New York Times, Al Jazeera, The Intercept, Wired, Mail & Guardian, and Truthdig. Across academic research and public writing, he explores how digital technology shapes empire, inequality, and ecological justice—and how it can instead serve people and the planet.
